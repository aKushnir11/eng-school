About Us

Founded by Ilona Danysh, our school is built on a foundation of academic excellence and extensive field experience. With both a Bachelor’s and a Master’s degree specializing in English Language Teaching, Ilona has dedicated years to the field of education. After gaining valuable experience in various public schools and kindergartens, she decided to create a modern, digital space where students could learn English comfortably and without stress.

In this way, we combine traditional pedagogy with a modern, more flexible approach that suits everyone. Our students have the opportunity to learn effectively and interactively, wherever they are. Real communication, practical skills, and constant support are the cornerstones of our lessons. We ensure that learning is clear, engaging, and tailored to each student’s individual goals. Our qualified and experienced teachers create a stimulating environment, adapting to any objective you may have.

Whether you are studying for work, travel, or personal development, we are here to guide you every step of the way.

Terms and Conditions of Study

General Lesson Policies

  • Format: lasses are conducted online via Google Meet or Zoom according to a pre-arranged schedule.
  • Duration: each session lasts 55 minutes (extensions are possible by mutual agreement).
  • Interaction: we highly recommend keeping your camera and microphone on to ensure effective engagement.
  • Cancellation: notice of cancellation must be provided at least 3 hours before the lesson starts. Otherwise, the session will be charged in full.

Rights and Obligations

Student’s Responsibilities

  • Regular attendance and punctuality.
  • Notice: providing advance notice for any absences.
  • Active Participation: interacting, asking questions, and completing tasks.
  • Preparation: completing homework to ensure better material retention.
  • Accountability: taking initiative and responsibility for learning results.

Teacher’s Responsibilities

  • Preparation: creating meaningful and engaging lessons.
  • Individual Approach: tailoring lessons to each student's level and needs.
  • Feedback: providing updates on progress and areas for improvement.
  • Environment: maintaining a friendly and comfortable learning atmosphere.
  • Development: constantly improving teaching skills and methodologies.
